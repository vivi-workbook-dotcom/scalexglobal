Our Process

The ScaleX Capital Framework

01

Capital Readiness Diagnostic

We audit your business from a lender's and investor's lens. Financials, project structure, collateral, promoter profile, and growth story. We find the gaps before the room does.

02

Raise Architecture

We define the right raise — amount, instrument (term loan, NCD, structured debt, equity, impact capital, government scheme), and lender/investor universe. No guesswork. No leaving money on the table.

03

Story Engineering

We craft your capital narrative — information memorandum, financial model, project brief, and promoter profile. Every document built to answer the questions that close mandates.

04

Lender & Investor Access

We map the right capital universe for your sector, stage, and geography. Banks. NBFCs. HFCs. PE funds. Impact investors. Family offices. Government schemes. We know who's deploying capital — and for what.

05

Deal Management & Close

We manage the entire process — introductions, follow-ups, due diligence support, and term negotiation guidance — until the funds hit your account.
