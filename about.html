The Founder's Story

Venkat Kumar Naidu

Founder, ScaleX Global

Twenty-five years.
Across industries, geographies, and roles that demanded everything.

From managing dealer networks across South India to owning P&L targets that kept you up at night. From building market entry strategy for construction equipment to structuring investor-ready documentation for large-scale capital raises.

Venkat Kumar Naidu has spent a career at the intersection of industry and capital — understanding not just what businesses should do, but how they actually get funded and built.

In association with SMC Corporation, he has advised on and closed over ₹710 Crores in capital raises — across real estate, infrastructure, and construction — for some of the most recognised names in South India's development sector.

He's been the person structuring the deal at midnight.
He's been the one in the lender's boardroom defending the project.
He's been the one who got the term sheet — when others said it wasn't possible.

ScaleX Global is the firm he wished existed when he needed it most.
